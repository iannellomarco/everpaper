Questions.

When does it take pictures of my screen? Open Close

Only while the journal is running, and you’re the one who starts it. It saves a picture of each screen every two minutes and reads the text on it, on your Mac. You can change the interval, pause whenever you like, and choose apps to skip: while one of those is in front, nothing is captured. It pauses on its own after two minutes away.

Does a bot join my meetings? Open Close

No. You press Record, or opt in and let your calendar start it. It records your microphone and your Mac’s system audio directly, so it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or anything else that plays through your Mac. Telling people they are being recorded is still up to you.

Where is my data kept? Open Close

On your Mac. Screenshots and the journal are files in your Library folder. By default a day’s screenshots and its written entry are deleted after 30 days; you can choose 7 days, 90 days or forever. Unless you switch on an online AI provider, the app goes online only to check for updates and to download its on-device models and the software that runs them.

Which AI does it use? Open Close

A model that runs on your Mac, installed during setup. Summaries and answers come from it, so you don’t need a ChatGPT plan or cloud compute. You can also use your own Ollama or LM Studio, or an online provider: Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini. Online AI stays off until you connect one and switch it on, and text that looks like a secret (API keys, tokens, card numbers, one-time codes) is removed before anything is sent.

Do I need an account? Open Close

No. There is nothing to sign in to. The app has no analytics and sends no usage data.

What do I need? Open Close

macOS 15 or later on an Apple-silicon Mac, plus a few gigabytes of free disk space for the speech and AI models.

How do I get it? Open Close

It’s in private beta. Request an invite with your email. If you’re selected, you hear back within 48 hours, plus a few short setup and feedback emails. No marketing email.
